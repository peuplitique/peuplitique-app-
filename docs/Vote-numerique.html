Digital Voting

Digital voting enables citizens to participate directly in collective decision-making through a transparent, secure and accessible system.

Using digital citizen identity and a citizen token, each participant is granted one unique voting right.

  • Citizen consultations
  • Referendums
  • Popular initiatives
  • Collective decisions
  • Thematic voting

Every vote is recorded in a verifiable manner while preserving voter confidentiality.

Digital voting is one of the key foundations of direct democracy and shared governance.
